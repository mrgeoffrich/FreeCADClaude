Mark up your FreeCAD views

  1. Ask Claude to send you a view In FreeCAD, ask for something like an isometric view of a named object to be sent to your device. It appears here.
  2. Draw on it Mark it up with a stylus or your finger, and place dimensions on the parts you want changed. Pinch with two fingers to zoom and pan.
  3. Tap Send Your marked-up image goes back to FreeCAD. Once you see it arrive, ask Claude to look at it and act on what you drew.

You can also mark up a photo or an image already on this device — tap the picture button at the top of the toolbar.
